Built an AI governance and enablement framework for the engineering organization. It is tool-agnostic by design, so adoption is governed by principles rather than tied to any single vendor, and it includes the context infrastructure that lets AI assistants work from shared engineering knowledge.

Evaluated emerging AI tools for testing and DevOps, balancing governance maturity with transparency about early-stage adoption, and prototypes agentic workflows hands-on.

Paired this with a delivery intelligence framework (predictability, capacity utilization, variance and stability) so decisions rest on evidence rather than anecdote.
